# DriverPass - System Analysis and Design Portfolio

## Project Reflection

### 1. Project Summary
**Client:** DriverPass (a small company providing driver training and education services).  
**System Type:** A web-based platform and automated management system designed to help student drivers prepare for their theoretical DMV exams and schedule practical, on-the-road training sessions. 

### 2. What I Did Particularly Well
I excelled at thoroughly gathering and organizing both functional and non-functional requirements into a clean Business Requirements Document. Translating raw client expectations into clear, structured parameters ensured that the technical scope was well-defined before moving into system design. 

### 3. Areas for Revision & Improvement
If I could revise one part of these documents, I would refine the UML Use Case and Activity Diagrams to better capture all complex interactions. In future projects, I would spend more time mapping out boundary conditions upfront to ensure every operational dependency is visually accounted for from the start.

### 4. Interpreting User Needs and System Design
I interpreted user needs by meticulously breaking down the client requirements into granular components to ensure no expectations were overlooked. Considering user needs is the single most critical factor in systems design because an application fails if it does not solve the client’s actual operational pain points.

### 5. Approach to Software Design & Future Strategies
My approach to designing software relies on a structured, top-down strategy: establishing a rigid requirements foundation, breaking high-level business goals into functional modules, and using visual UML modeling to map out object relationships clearly before implementation.
